FAQs

Is there any account opening charge on Integrated?

No. Opening a Demat & Trading Account with Integrated is completely free. There are no account opening charges, so you can get started with your investment journey without any upfront fees.

What are the Monthly Maintenance Charges (MMC)?

The Monthly Maintenance Charge (MMC) is ₹25 per month. However, these charges are not applicable for BSDA (Basic Services Demat Account) eligible clients.

What brokerage is charged on equity trades?

Brokerage varies based on the type of trade and trading mode. Different brokerage rates apply to equity delivery, intraday, futures, and options for online assisted trading. You can refer to the brokerage tables above for complete details.

Are GST and other statutory charges included in the brokerage?

No. Brokerage charges are exclusive of GST and other applicable statutory and regulatory charges such as exchange transaction charges, SEBI charges, stamp duty, and depository charges. These are charged separately wherever applicable.

Can the brokerage and service charges change?

Yes. Brokerage and other applicable charges are subject to change in line with regulatory requirements and company policies. Any revisions will be updated accordingly.
