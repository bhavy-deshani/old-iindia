NSE IPO Opens September 17: Price Band, Financials and What Investors Should Know

IPO IPO Investment Stock Market
Share now

The long wait for the National Stock Exchange of India’s IPO is finally nearing an end. After receiving approval from the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI), NSE has now announced a price band of ₹1,700 to ₹1,785 per share for its much-awaited public issue.

The upcoming IPO is scheduled to open for subscription on September 17, 2026, and close on September 21, 2026. The issue is valued at ₹22,561.57 crore, making it one of the largest IPOs in India's capital markets.

The IPO is entirely an Offer for Sale (OFS), meaning NSE itself will not receive any of the money raised from the issue. Existing shareholders will sell their shares to the public. The final price band is now known, so investors can assess the issue based on NSE's financial performance, valuation, market position and the latest IPO details.

Why is the NSE IPO Creating So Much Interest?

NSE is not an ordinary company entering the stock market. It is India's largest stock exchange by trading volumes and plays a major role across equity, derivatives, currency trading, clearing, settlement, indices and market data.

The IPO will involve approximately 12.64 crore shares. Since the issue is entirely an OFS, NSE will not receive the IPO proceeds. Instead, the funds raised will go to the shareholders selling their stakes.

The IPO has a price band of ₹1,700 to ₹1,785 per share, with a lot size of 8 shares. At the upper end of the price band, a retail investor would need a minimum investment of ₹14,280 for one lot.

What Should Investors Check Before Applying for the NSE IPO?

The NSE name and its position in India's financial markets may naturally attract investor attention. However, the decision should go beyond the brand.

Investors should consider:

  • Valuation: Does the IPO price adequately reflect NSE's earnings and growth prospects?
  • Financial performance: NSE's FY26 profit declined from the previous year, although Q1 FY27 showed year-on-year growth.
  • Market position: NSE has a strong position across India's equity and derivatives markets.
  • Business concentration: Investors should understand how much of NSE's revenue is linked to different trading segments.
  • Regulatory environment: Changes in trading rules and derivatives regulations can affect exchange revenues.
  • OFS structure: Since this is entirely an OFS, NSE itself will not receive the IPO proceeds.

Investors who decide to participate can apply for the issue through the IPO online process using their broker or other permitted application platforms. Before submitting an application, it is important to check the lot size, required investment amount, price band and other IPO details.

Conclusion

The NSE IPO has moved from being a long-awaited possibility to a confirmed public issue. With the IPO scheduled to open on September 17, 2026, investors now have access to the key details needed to evaluate the offering.

The ₹1,700 to ₹1,785 price band, approximately ₹22,561.57 crore issue size, 8-share lot size and OFS structure provide a much clearer picture than was available when the IPO was first approved.

NSE continues to have a strong position in India's capital markets and has reported healthy Q1 FY27 growth. At the same time, its FY26 profit was lower than FY25, and the valuation at the upper price band remains an important factor to assess.

For investors considering the issue, the focus should therefore remain on valuation, financial performance, business strength and long-term prospects, rather than GMP or the popularity of the NSE brand alone.
